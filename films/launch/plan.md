# Quillon Briefs — launch film plan (lane D)

**One line:** Quillon now hands you the finished document — a client alert, a board memo, a chronology exhibit — not a chat answer.
**Viewer:** a partner or senior associate who already trusts Quillon's answers and still spends the evening formatting them.
**Structure:** before/after → reveal → proof → range → CTA (story spine).
**Proof moment:** b6 — the finished two-page client alert lands on the beat drop; the pointer clicks footnote 1 and the cited clause opens beside it.
**Tempo:** 100 BPM, 4/4 → beat 0.6 s, bar 2.4 s; cuts on the half-bar grid (1.2 s). Drop at 24.0 s (bar 11).
**Length:** 50.4 s (21 bars).
